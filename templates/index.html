How it works

  1. Sign up with a username and password.
  2. Pick a mission and open phase 1.
  3. Find the flag. It looks like flag{some_text}.
  4. Submit it to get the points and unlock the next phase.
  5. If you get stuck, each phase has 3 hints.

Everything is saved to your account. Log in from any computer to carry on where you left off, and see where you rank on the scoreboard.

Tools you'll want

  • Your browser's dev tools (F12)
  • CyberChef for decoding text and looking inside files
  • Python 3
